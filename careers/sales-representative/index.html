Sales Representative

Accra 3-month contract Field sales
Apply by email
Share: Link copied

We're looking for a confident, self-driven Sales Representative to bring landlords and property managers in Accra onto ConciergeGh. You'll find prospects, run demos and site visits, and sign them up, working to clear weekly and monthly targets.

What you'll do

  • Find and contact landlords, property managers, hostel and apartment owners across Accra.
  • Explain how ConciergeGh works and run demos (in person and on the phone).
  • Visit properties, meet owners and caretakers, and follow up until they sign up.
  • Help new clients get started on the app.
  • Log every lead, call, visit and follow-up in our sales app (you'll get a partner account and training).
  • Meet your weekly and monthly targets.

Targets (deliverables)

You'll have clear targets, reviewed weekly and at the end of each month — for example:

  • New landlords contacted each week
  • Follow-up conversations each week
  • Demos and site visits each week
  • Paying landlords signed each month

Exact numbers are shared at interview.

What you get

  • GHS 1,500 / month base pay
  • Commission on every paying landlord you sign, paid to your MoMo
  • Transport, lunch and airtime allowances
  • Training on the product and on selling it
  • A 3-month contract with target deliverables

What we're looking for

  • Experience in sales, real estate, field marketing or customer-facing work (a plus, not a must).
  • Confident speaking to people on the phone and face to face; English and Twi (other Ghanaian languages a plus).
  • Organised, self-motivated and comfortable working to targets.
  • Good knowledge of Accra and able to move around the city.
  • A smartphone and basic comfort with apps.
