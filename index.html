Why ConciergeGh

A single system for the whole portfolio.

Replace paper, spreadsheets, and memory with one digital system that the whole family can use — securely, with full accountability.

Status at a glance

Dashboard shows rent collected, overdue totals, units due this week, and occupancy — in one screen.

Automated reminders

SMS and WhatsApp reminders go out 3 days before rent is due, on the due date, and after overdue — automatically.

Full audit trail

Mobile-first

Seven modules. One app.

Everything you need to run a portfolio.

From a single building to a portfolio spread across cities — ConciergeGh handles one property or many from the same dashboard.

1

Dashboard

At-a-glance health: rent collected this month, overdue totals, units due this week, occupancy rate, and the last five actions across the team.

2

Rent Tracker

Every unit, every month — with Paid / Due / Overdue badges. Record a payment (Cash or MoMo) in two taps. Tap any row for full history.

3

Tenant Records

A directory of every tenant — name, unit, phone, rent, lease dates, status. Vacant units shown clearly. Every edit logged.

4

Income & Expense Ledger

Full financial log with categories, month/year filters, and one-click CSV export. Payments auto-flow into income.

5

Maintenance Log

Log a repair request against any unit with priority (Low / Medium / High), track status to resolution, and see who marked it done.

6

Multi-Property Management

Manage multiple separate properties from one account — apartments in Accra, flats in Kumasi, family homes in Tema. Filter any view by property or see them all together.

7

Tenant Portal

For tenants

A dedicated login for your tenants — so they're part of the system, not chasing you on WhatsApp.

Report issues — submit maintenance requests with photos, straight from their phone
Digitally sign tenancy agreements — no printing, scanning, or hand-delivery
View payment history — every cedi they've paid, with receipts on demand
Rent reminders & receipts — automatic notifications and downloadable proof of payment
