Short Stays & Bookings

Let rooms and apartments night-by-night, Airbnb-style, and manage every guest right inside ConciergeGh — no separate app, no separate books.

Flexible nightly pricing

Switch any vacant unit to short stays and set nightly, weekend and seasonal rates, a cleaning fee, minimum nights and a refundable deposit.

Calendar, no double bookings

A booking calendar and list view for every unit. Overlapping dates are blocked automatically, so a room is never sold twice.

Payments & deposits

Record full payments, part-payments and deposits with balances tracked. Short-stay income flows straight into your ledger.

Check-in & check-out

Handle check-ins and check-outs with deposit refunds or deductions, and process cancellations with refunds in a few taps.

Guest reminders

Optional booking confirmation, check-in and balance reminders to guests by SMS or email, using editable templates.

Live short-stay dashboard

See today's arrivals and departures, short-stay income, occupancy and your average nightly rate at a glance.

Supervisor alerts

Get notified on new bookings, payments, check-ins, check-outs and cancellations as they happen.

Partner Program Terms
  1. Eligibility: Anyone 18+ with a valid Ghana Mobile Money number can join. One account per person.
  2. Earning: You earn 10% of the first subscription payment made by a landlord who signs up using your referral code or link. No commission on later renewals, SMS/email bundles or other services.
  3. Valid referrals: The landlord must enter your code (or use your link) when creating their account. Codes can't be added after sign-up unless ConciergeGh agrees. Self-referrals (your own or a household member's account) don't count.
  4. When you're paid: Commission becomes payable the month after the landlord's payment clears. You can request a payout once your balance reaches GHS 50. Payouts are sent to your registered Mobile Money number after review.
  5. Refunds: If a landlord's payment is refunded within 30 days, the related commission is cancelled or deducted from your balance.
  6. Fair play: No spam, fake accounts, misleading claims about ConciergeGh, or paid ads using the ConciergeGh name without permission. Breaking these rules can lead to removal from the program and loss of unpaid commission.
  7. Changes: ConciergeGh may update the commission rate or these terms, or end the program, with notice. Commission already earned will still be paid.
  8. Taxes: You are responsible for any taxes on your earnings.
  9. Privacy: Partners can see the business names and number of landlords who signed up with their code — not their tenants, rent or financial details.
  10. Questions: Contact ConciergeGh support on WhatsApp.
